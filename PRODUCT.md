# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Harla Hotel guests booking rooms from Ethiopia and abroad, often on mobile devices and with varying familiarity with online hotel booking. Their primary task is to choose an available room, enter trustworthy guest details, understand the total price, and complete the appropriate payment path without uncertainty.

## Product Purpose

The booking flow connects Harla Hotel's live Supabase room inventory to a clear four-step reservation process. Success means guests can submit accurate, verifiable bookings while the hotel receives consistent records and secure supporting documents for review.

## Brand Personality

Warm, refined, and trustworthy. The experience should feel grounded in Harari hospitality while meeting the expectations of a professional international hotel booking service.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic travel templates, oversized decorative layouts, confusing multi-purpose forms, unverified payment claims, and interfaces that expose technical database language to guests.

## Design Principles

- Make the next booking decision obvious at every step.
- Explain validation and payment requirements in plain language.
- Preserve trust through accurate availability, pricing, and status messaging.
- Use Harla Hotel's black and gold identity with restraint on task-focused screens.
- Treat mobile, keyboard, and error-state behavior as core booking functionality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic labels, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, status announcements, mobile-responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Do not rely on color alone to communicate validation or payment state.
