# Harla restaurant food showcase

All 20 photographs were supplied by the owner on 25 September 2026.
Original filenames are preserved as lowercase WebP filenames. These are real
restaurant photos, not generated images. No dishes, people or furnishings were
added or removed. `IMG_1246.JPG` and `IMG_20260507_125016_575.JPG` were rotated
90 degrees counterclockwise to correct their orientation. Images were resized
within 1440 × 1440 without enlargement and encoded as WebP at quality 85.

The homepage places the showcase immediately before restaurant order options.
The slideshow leads with a curated selection; the expandable gallery exposes
all 20 photos. Only the current/next full photograph is loaded for the slideshow;
gallery images are lazy-loaded when opened. Motion stops offscreen, on hover,
on keyboard focus and in hidden tabs. Reduced-motion preferences disable
automatic playback and transitions. Customers can pause, use arrow keys, swipe,
or select a thumbnail. Captions describe the photos without assigning menu prices.
